# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Linotes to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind, patient and respectful, especially with newcomers
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes and learning from them

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or private
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances
- Trolling or deliberately derailing discussions
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions and chat —
and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit or
reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban contributors, for behaviour they deem
inappropriate.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the project maintainers. Until a dedicated contact
address is published, use GitHub's private reporting channel for the repository or contact a
maintainer directly. All reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.

Maintainers will follow these steps when deciding on consequences:

1. **Correction** — a private, written warning explaining why the behaviour was inappropriate.
2. **Warning** — a warning with consequences for continued behaviour.
3. **Temporary ban** — no interaction with the community for a specified period.
4. **Permanent ban** — for a pattern of violations or severe misconduct.
